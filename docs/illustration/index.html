Select unit k in hidden layer l. Its highlighted connections form one vector 𝐰kl. The three priors differ in how they assign its scale λkl.

Each of the dl=Kl−1+1 coefficients has conditional standard deviation σλkl. Given the scales, the coefficients are independent. The same σ2 is the observation variance in the likelihood

Yi∣𝐱i,𝒲,σ2~𝒩(f𝒲(𝐱i),σ2).
Prior on the observation variance

The study uses p(σ2)∝1/σ2 on the standardized response scale. Posterior propriety holds for almost every response direction under the paper’s conditions. For a given data set, a finite variational fit alone cannot establish propriety. To use the supplement’s proper inverse-gamma alternative in R, set noise_prior = c(shape = 2, scale = 1), or supply other positive hyperparameters.

Fitted scales · Model comparison

From shrinkage to pruning

Ghosh, Yao and Doshi-Velez group the same incoming coefficients as our GL-BNN. In their notation τkl is local and υl is global. Their post-fitting rule removes unit k if

Prq(τklυl<δ)>p0.

Here q is their fitted variational distribution, δ=10−3, and p0=0.9. In the grouped-horseshoe example, move the hypothetical fitted probability across the threshold.

For our models, Prq(σλkl<ϵ) summarizes mass near a small conditional weight SD. All Kl units remain in the fitted architecture. ReLU homogeneity allows an outgoing weight to compensate for a rescaling of the incoming weights and bias.
