Fitted scales · Model comparison

Pruning unit 2 in layer 1

Ghosh, Yao and Doshi-Velez group the same 4 incoming coefficients as our GL-BNN. In their notation τ21 is the unit’s local scale and υ1 is the shared layer scale. Their post-fitting rule removes unit 2 if

Prq(τ21υ1<δ)>p0.

Here q is their fitted variational distribution, δ=10−3, and p0=0.9. In the grouped-horseshoe example, move the hypothetical fitted probability across the threshold.

For our models, Prq(σλ21<ϵ) summarizes mass near a small conditional weight SD. All 4 units remain in layer 1 of the fitted architecture. ReLU homogeneity allows an outgoing weight to compensate for a rescaling of the incoming weights and bias.
