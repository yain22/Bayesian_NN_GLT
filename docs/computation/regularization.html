Full fitting objectives for the selected networks

At fixed noise variance, a Laplace prior gives an L₁ penalty in the negative log posterior. Matching the penalty strength gives a posterior mode. The three Bayesian fits here estimate distributions for the weights and shrinkage scales.

What the fits have in common

Diameter and height enter a ReLU network with three units in the first hidden layer and two in the second. Every fifth tree in datasets::trees is held out. The remaining 25 trees supply the standardization and the common starting weights, obtained with 600 squared-error updates (seed 7). Each FNN then takes 600 Adam steps. Each BNN attempts ten weight updates per cycle for 60 cycles, alternating with scale updates. These are separate matched fits for this comparison.
